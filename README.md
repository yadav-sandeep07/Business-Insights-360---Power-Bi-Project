## 📌 Project Overview

Business Insights 360 is an interactive Power BI project designed to provide a comprehensive view of business performance through department-specific dashboards and key performance indicators (KPIs).

The solution brings business data together to help stakeholders understand profitability, analyze sales trends, evaluate marketing performance, and monitor supply chain efficiency. It demonstrates how data analytics can transform raw data into meaningful insights for better business decisions.

## 🎯 Business Objectives

* Develop a centralized view of business performance.
* Analyze revenue, costs, gross margin, and profitability.
* Identify top-performing products, customers, and sales channels.
* Evaluate market share and marketing performance.
* Monitor forecast accuracy and supply chain efficiency.
* Enable interactive analysis using filters and drill-downs.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

## 🗂️ Data Modeling

Developed a structured data model to integrate business datasets and support consistent reporting across multiple business functions.

**Key data areas:**

* Sales transactions and forecast data
* Product and customer information
* Calendar and fiscal date dimensions
* Manufacturing and freight costs
* Financial metrics and invoice deductions
* Market share and target data

The data model supports meaningful relationships, consistent KPI calculations, and interactive cross-filtering across report pages.

## 📊 Dashboard Modules

### 💰 1. Finance View

Provides insights into financial performance and profitability.

* Net Sales and Cost of Goods Sold (COGS)
* Gross Margin and Gross Margin %
* Net Profit analysis
* Financial performance trends

### 🛒 2. Sales View

Helps evaluate sales performance across customers, products, and channels.

* Customer and product performance
* Net Sales contribution
* Channel-wise analysis
* Sales trends and growth opportunities

### 📣 3. Marketing View

Focuses on market performance and profitability.

* Market share analysis
* Product and regional performance
* Profitability metrics
* Target achievement tracking

### 🚚 4. Supply Chain View

Monitors forecast performance and demand planning.

* Forecast Accuracy %
* Net Error
* Absolute Error
* Forecast versus actual sales analysis

### 👔 5. Executive View

Offers a consolidated overview of key business metrics.

* High-level business KPIs
* Overall sales and profitability
* Performance trends
* Insights to support strategic decisions



## 📈 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

| KPI                 | Description                                            |
| ------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------ |
| Net Sales           | Measures sales revenue after applicable deductions     |
| Gross Margin %      | Measures gross profitability relative to net sales     |
| Net Profit          | Indicates profit after applicable expenses             |
| Forecast Accuracy % | Evaluates forecast performance against actual demand   |
| Absolute Error      | Measures the magnitude of forecast deviations          |
| Net Error           | Indicates the direction and magnitude of forecast bias |
